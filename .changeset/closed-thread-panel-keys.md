In the web app, ← in a closed thread's panel moves focus to the channel feed's "N closed threads" fold that holds the thread, and Escape there closes the panel with focus on that fold, so j and k carry on from it. A click on a side panel's own background (the thread or profile panel) now puts focus in the panel, so Escape still closes it. Closing a thread lands on its channel message, or on the channel's newest message when that one isn't loaded; at a narrow width, Escape from the Inbox's conversation lands on its row, and j/k carry on from the focused row. ← at the very start of a message or reply box (or in an empty one) moves to the column on the left, keeping what you typed; anywhere else in the box it moves the caret.
