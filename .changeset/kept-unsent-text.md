The web message box and reply box keep the text typed in them and not sent yet, separately for each channel and each thread: leave and come back, or reload the page, and it is still there with the caret at its end. A thread's reply box in the thread panel and in the Inbox share it. It is kept in this browser only, for the identity logged in, never on the server; files added and not sent aren't kept. Sending the message, or emptying the box, forgets it.
