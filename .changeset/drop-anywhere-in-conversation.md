In the web UI, files dropped anywhere on a channel, a thread or an Inbox conversation (its messages, its box or the hint under it) wait in that conversation's box, and the conversation lights up while files are dragged over it. With a thread open, a drop on the thread goes to its reply box and one on the channel to the channel's. Files dropped anywhere else are refused, so the browser never opens them in a tab. A paste whose file is only among the clipboard's items now attaches it too.
