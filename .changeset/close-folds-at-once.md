In the web app, a thread you close folds away into "N closed threads" at once, even with something unread for you in it, and focus on its channel message moves to the next message, else the one before it. Closed from its thread panel, the panel stays open, labelled Closed, while the channel behind it folds the thread away; ← or Escape from that panel (or any closed thread's) lands on the channel message after the fold holding it, else before it, else the fold. Reopening brings the thread back into the channel in place. A thread someone else closed, with something unread for you, still stays out of the fold while the channel is open.
