In the web app, a long message (taller than about 12 lines) shows its first 8 lines fading out, in a channel, the thread panel and the Inbox conversation, unless it mentions you, you wrote it, it's below the "New" line, or you opened it from a link or the Inbox. Enter on a folded message unfolds it in place, as does clicking its faded end, and a screen reader hears "Expanded"; Enter on an unfolded or short message, and → on any message, still opens its thread at the reply box. An unfolded message stays unfolded until you leave the page.
